Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

VATchecker is a client-side tool. The revenue figures, business type, and country you enter are processed entirely in your browser — they are not sent to our servers or stored anywhere except your own device.

The only data sent externally is:

  • A request to Google Fonts to load the Inter typeface.
  • A request to euvatrates.com to fetch the latest EU VAT rates (anonymous, no personal data).
  • For EU VAT number validation: a request to the European Commission VIES API containing only the VAT number you submit.

Local storage

The invoice generator saves your draft invoice to your browser's localStorage so you can return to it later. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clicking Reset in the invoice generator, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising and cookies

VATchecker uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. This may include personalized advertising depending on your settings and location.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalization by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent message giving the choice to accept or decline personalized advertising, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy.

For more detail on how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Analytics

We do not currently run any first-party analytics or tracking scripts on this site, beyond the advertising cookies described above.

Third-party links

This site links to official government tax authority websites and other external resources. These services have their own privacy policies.
