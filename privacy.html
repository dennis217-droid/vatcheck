Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

VATcheck is a client-side tool. The revenue figures, business type, and country you enter are processed entirely in your browser — they are not sent to our servers or stored anywhere except your own device.

The only data sent externally is:

  • A request to Google Fonts to load the Inter and Syne typefaces.
  • A request to euvatrates.com to fetch the latest EU VAT rates (anonymous, no personal data).
  • For EU VAT number validation: a request to the European Commission VIES API containing only the VAT number you submit.

Local storage

The invoice generator saves your draft invoice to your browser's localStorage so you can return to it later. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clicking Reset in the invoice generator, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookies

VATcheck does not use cookies. Your dark mode preference and invoice data are stored in localStorage only.

Analytics

We do not currently run any analytics or tracking scripts on this site.

Third-party links

This site links to external services including FreshBooks, Xero, Zoho Books, Sage, Moneybird, and Lexoffice. These are affiliate links — we may earn a commission if you sign up. These services have their own privacy policies.
