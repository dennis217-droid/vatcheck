About VATchecker

Free, independent tools that help freelancers and small businesses make sense of VAT and GST — without the jargon.

Why we built this

Figuring out whether you need to register for VAT or GST shouldn't require an accountant and an afternoon. Registration thresholds, rates, and filing rules vary wildly between countries and change every year — so we built a set of free tools that give small business owners and freelancers a quick, plain-English answer, backed by official government sources.

What VATchecker offers

VAT / GST threshold checker VAT invoice generator Reverse VAT calculator VAT number validator Country VAT/GST guide

How we keep data accurate

Rates and thresholds are sourced directly from official tax authority websites (HMRC, ATO, CRA, BZSt, Revenue.ie, and others) and verified periodically. EU VAT number validation uses the European Commission's live VIES API. That said, tax rules change — always confirm anything important with the official source or a qualified accountant.
